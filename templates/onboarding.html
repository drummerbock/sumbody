HOW IT WORKS

Practice becoming the heartbeat.

Use rhythm to notice where you are moving, establish the patterns you want to return to, record what you actually do, and see what those actions create over time.

01

Life Areas

Where your movement lives. Choose the parts of your life you want to pay attention to — from Family or Work to a hobby, a relationship, a side hustle, or a cause.

Manage Life Areas →
02

Rhythmos

The rhythmic movements you want to establish. Give a Life Area a pattern you want to keep returning to throughout your day.

Open Rhythmos →
03

Beats

What you actually do. Each meaningful action you record is one Beat. Small Beats matter because rhythm is built through repetition.

04

Accents

Some Beats carry more emphasis. Accent a Beat when that action deserves to stand out from the surrounding rhythm.

05

Today's Tempo

The rhythm of what is happening now. Today's Tempo shows the Beats and Accents shaping your current day.

Open Today's Tempo →
06

Audify

Make the pattern perceptible. See what your accumulated actions reveal through insights, then look underneath those insights at the individual Beats themselves.

Open Audify →
RESONANCE

What continues beyond the Beat

Repeated action creates rhythm. Rhythm can resonate through you, your relationships, and the people around you. Over time, the practice is not simply to record a heartbeat — it is to become one.

Explore the musical language →
