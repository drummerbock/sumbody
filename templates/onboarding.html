HOW IT WORKS

Practice becoming the heartbeat.

SUMbody uses the language of rhythm to help you notice where you are moving, what you are trying to establish, and what your repeated actions are actually creating.

01

Life Areas

Where your movement lives. Choose the parts of your life you want to pay attention to—from Family or Work to a hobby, a relationship, a side hustle, or a cause.

02

Pulse

What you want to carry into that movement. In music, pulse is the steady underlying recurrence that gives music forward motion. In SUMbody, it is the intention or direction underneath what you are trying to build.

03

Rhythmos

The rhythm you intend to establish. A Rhythmos gives one Life Area a recurring pattern you want to strengthen and return to.

04

Beats

What you actually do. In music, the beat is a basic unit of time. Here, each meaningful action you record is one Beat.

05

Accents

Some Beats carry more emphasis. In music, an accent gives extra emphasis to a beat without changing the beat itself. In SUMbody, accent a Beat when that action carried unusual significance.

06

Audify

Make the pattern perceptible. Repeated Beats form rhythm. Audify lets you look back at what actually happened so the pattern you are creating becomes easier to see.

07

Resonance

What your rhythm affects beyond the individual Beat. In music, resonance is the continuation or amplification of vibration. In life, repeated actions can resonate through you, your relationships, and the people around you.
