You do not need to learn a complicated system. Start by noticing what you do. Over time, the pattern becomes visible.

Beat

A Beat is one meaningful action. Called someone you love. Took a walk. Finished something difficult. Helped a coworker. Start small.

02

Today's Tempo

Your Beats tell the story of how you are actually moving through today. Do not plan the perfect day. Record the one you are living.

03

Rhythmos

Rhythmos is the rhythm you intentionally want to establish or strengthen. Name the patterns you want to create in the parts of life that matter to you.

04

Audify

One Beat is an action. Repeated Beats become rhythm. Audify makes that rhythm perceptible so you can see what your actions are actually creating.

You do not need to become someone different.
