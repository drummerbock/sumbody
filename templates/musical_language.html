THE MUSICAL LANGUAGE

The words behind the framework

SUMbody borrows from music because rhythm gives us a precise way to talk about repeated action, emphasis, movement, perception, and effect.

Beat

What you did
In music

A basic unit of musical time—the recurring point you can feel or count.

In SUMbody

One meaningful action. A Beat is the smallest recorded unit of what you actually did.

Pulse

What drives the movement
In music

The steady underlying recurrence that gives music a sense of forward motion, even when every pulse is not explicitly played.

In SUMbody

The intention or inner direction you want to carry into a part of your life.

Rhythm

The pattern that emerges
In music

The pattern created by sounds, silences, durations, and accents as they unfold over the pulse.

In SUMbody

The pattern your repeated Beats create over time. Rhythm is not one action; it emerges from repetition.

Rhythmos

The pattern you intend to build
In music

Rhythmos comes from the Greek root associated with rhythm, measured flow, and patterned movement.

In SUMbody

A recurring pattern you intentionally want to establish or strengthen inside a Life Area.

Tempo

Your present pace
In music

The speed of the pulse—how quickly the beats occur.

In SUMbody

The immediate pace and shape of your activity. Today's Tempo is the view of what is happening now.

Accent

Extra emphasis
In music

Extra emphasis placed on a particular note or beat without changing the underlying beat itself.

In SUMbody

A marker that one particular Beat carried unusual significance. The Beat remains the action; the Accent tells you it stood out.

Dynamics

Degrees of intensity
In music

The relative level of loudness or intensity, expressed with markings such as pianissimo, mezzo forte, and fortissimo.

In SUMbody

The broader idea that emphasis can have degrees. Dynamics may help describe stronger or softer Accents as the framework develops.

Movement

Where life takes shape
In music

A substantial, self-contained section of a larger composition, often with its own character while still belonging to the whole.

In SUMbody

Life in motion. Each Life Area can be thought of as one place where your larger life is actively taking shape.

Life Area

Where your movement lives
In music

Life Area is not a formal musical term; its closest analogy is a movement, section, voice, or part within a larger work.

In SUMbody

The part of your life where Beats occur: Family, Work, a particular relationship, a hobby, a side hustle, a cause, or anything else meaningful to you.

Audify

Make the pattern perceptible
In sound and data

Audification is the act of rendering information as sound so that a pattern can be perceived through listening.

In SUMbody

Look back at accumulated Beats so the rhythm you are actually creating becomes perceptible.

Resonance

What continues beyond the Beat
In music

The continuation or amplification of vibration; one vibrating body can also cause another to respond.

In SUMbody

The effect your repeated actions have beyond the individual moment—on you, other people, relationships, and your environment.

Heartbeat

The pattern you become
In rhythm

A heartbeat is a literal biological pulse and one of the most fundamental human models of steady recurring time.

In SUMbody

The larger metaphor for a stable, recognizable way of moving through life. You become the Heartbeat when your values repeatedly become observable action.

THE FRAMEWORK

Life Area → Pulse → Rhythmos → Beat → Accent → Rhythm → Audify → Resonance

Where → intention → intended pattern → action → emphasis → emerging pattern → perception → effect
