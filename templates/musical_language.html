THE MUSICAL LANGUAGE

The words behind the practice

Becoming the Heartbeat borrows from music because rhythm gives us a precise way to talk about pace, movement, repeated action, emphasis, perception, and effect.

Tempo

Your present pace
In music

The speed at which music proceeds — the rate of the beat.

In this practice

The current pace and pattern of your activity. Today's Tempo shows the rhythm your actions are creating now.

Rhythmos

Dimensions of your life
Origin

Rhythmos comes from the ancient Greek root associated with measured movement, flow, and recurring form. It is the root from which the word rhythm developed.

In this practice

A dimension of your life in which your rhythm develops, such as Family, Work, a relationship, a hobby, or a cause. It does not need to be a routine or a goal.

Audify

Make the pattern perceptible
In sound and data

Audification presents information directly as sound so changes and patterns can be perceived through listening.

In this practice

Step back from individual Beats so the rhythm your accumulated actions are creating becomes perceptible.

Beat

What you did
In music

A regular unit of musical time that listeners can feel and performers can count.

In this practice

One meaningful action. A Beat is the smallest recorded unit of what you actually did.

Accent

Extra emphasis
In music

Emphasis placed on a particular note or beat relative to what surrounds it.

In this practice

A marker that one particular Beat carried unusual significance. The Beat remains the action; the Accent tells you it stood out.

Rhythm

The pattern that emerges
In music

The organization of sounds and silences in time, including their duration, placement, and emphasis.

In this practice

The pattern your repeated Beats create over time. Rhythm is not one action; it emerges from repetition.

Movement

Life in motion
In music

A substantial, self-contained section of a larger composition, often with its own character while still belonging to the whole.

In this practice

Life in motion. A movement is a pattern taking shape through repeated action.

Resonance

What continues beyond the Beat
In sound

The strengthening or prolonging of vibration when a system responds to vibration at particular frequencies.

In this practice

The effect your repeated actions have beyond the individual moment — on you, other people, relationships, and your environment.

Heartbeat

The pattern you become
In rhythm

A heartbeat is a biological pulse and one of the most fundamental human examples of recurring time.

In this practice

The larger metaphor for a stable, recognizable way of moving through life. You become the Heartbeat when your values repeatedly become observable action.
